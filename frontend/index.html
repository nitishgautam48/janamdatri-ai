If you notice any of these, get help now

Tap a sign to learn what to do. You don't need an account to call for help.

Heavy Bleedingज़्यादा खून बहना

Bleeding that soaks a pad, or comes with clots or pain. Lie down and go to the nearest hospital now.

Call 108Nearest hospital
Reduced Fetal Movementबच्चे की हलचल कम

Baby moving much less than usual after week 28. Lie on your left side, count kicks, and contact your ASHA or facility.

Call 108Nearest hospital
Severe Headacheतेज़ सिरदर्द

A headache that won't go away, especially with blurred vision or swelling, can mean high BP. Get checked today.

Call 108Nearest hospital
Convulsionsदौरे पड़ना

Fits or shaking of the body. Turn her on her side, keep her safe, and call 108 immediately.

Call 108Nearest hospital
Breathing Difficultyसाँस लेने में तकलीफ़

Struggling to breathe at rest, or chest pain. Sit upright and get emergency help.

Call 108Nearest hospital
High Feverतेज़ बुखार

Fever with chills or foul-smelling discharge. Drink fluids and visit your PHC the same day.

Call 108Nearest hospital
